FaceBlur detects and blurs the faces in your photos before you share them. Everything happens on your device: no network access, no tracking, no photo ever leaves your phone.

• Automatic on-device face detection (YuNet model)
• Gaussian blur or mosaic, with adjustable strength and tile size
• Tap to exclude or restore individual faces; draw extra zones manually
• Zoom and pan to check every detail
• Export as JPEG, PNG or WebP
• Metadata removed by default; optional keeping of date, device and title
• Offline, in French, English, German, Italian and Spanish

The face detection model is YuNet from the OpenCV Zoo (MIT licence), embedded in the app and verified by checksum; its full text is available in the app's credits screen.
